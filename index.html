About

Why we started Elen

[EDIT ME] Most luxury sunglasses are made in the same few factories as everyone else's, then sold on the strength of a logo. We wanted to pay for the frame instead. Elen pairs are cut from Italian acetate, polished by hand and fitted with polarised lenses.

[EDIT ME] We make each frame in runs of a few hundred. When a colour sells out, it may come back next season, or it may not. That's the deal with doing it this way.
