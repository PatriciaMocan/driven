About

Why we started Elen

[EDIT ME] I spent years buying sunglasses that were either cheap and fell apart by August, or expensive mostly because of the name printed on the arm. Elen is my attempt at the pair in between. Acetate frames, polarised lenses, proper hinges. Nothing else on them.

[EDIT ME] We work with one small workshop and order in short runs, so we don't end up with warehouses of stock nobody wanted. The first collection is six frames. We'd rather get six right than rush out thirty.
